# What changes by channel

The shape in the skill body is constant. These are the differences that decide
whether it gets read.

## Freelance platform bid (Upwork, Malt, freelancermap, Contra)

- **120–200 words.** The client is reading twenty of these in a list view; only
  the first two lines are visible before they click.
- **Lead with the outcome, not the greeting.** No "Dear Hiring Manager".
- **Answer their screening questions literally**, in their order. Platforms
  surface these; skipping one reads as a template.
- **Availability and rate go in.** Platforms filter on them, and omitting them
  is read as "will be expensive".
- **No attachments unless asked.** They are rarely opened at this stage.

## Cover letter for a permanent role

- **Under one page.** Three or four short paragraphs.
- **Name the company and something true about it** — a product decision, a
  technical post they published. One clause, not a paragraph, and never
  flattery you cannot support.
- **The gap paragraph matters more here.** Permanent hiring reads for risk.
- **Match their formality.** A Berlin startup and a Mittelstand company are not
  the same document.

## Cold email to a named person

- **Under 120 words**, and the subject line does the work.
- **Say why them.** A cold email with no specific reason to have picked this
  person is a mailshot, and reads like one.
- **One ask, small.** A 20-minute call, not a job.
- **No attachment.** A link, at most.

## CV variant

- **Reorder, re-weight, never invent.** Same facts every time.
- **Top third decides it.** The roles and bullets that match this posting go
  above the fold; everything else compresses.
- **Mirror their nouns** so keyword filters and human skimmers both find them.
- **One page per five years**, and never more than two.

## Follow-up message

- **Add something, do not just bump.** A relevant link, an answer you thought
  of afterwards, a change in your availability.
- **Reference the specific date and conversation.** "Following up on our call
  on the 3rd about the ingestion pipeline."
- **State the deadline if one is real.** Another offer, a contract ending.
- **Two, then stop.** The cadence table in the `application-pipeline` skill has
  the timing.
