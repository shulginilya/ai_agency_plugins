# Reply cadence

How fast to answer, when to follow up, when to stop. Propose dates from this
table, state them, and write them only when the user agrees.

## Answering

| Situation | Answer within |
| --- | --- |
| A recruiter or hiring manager asks a direct question | 1 working day |
| They proposed times for a call | same day — slots go stale |
| A first-contact message that fits | 2 working days |
| A qualify message (too little said) | 3 working days |
| A decline | within a week; late is fine, never is not |

A reply owed for more than three working days is the one thing worth raising
at the start of a session. After a week, answer anyway and do not apologise at
length — one clause is enough.

## Following up on our message

| We sent | Follow up after | Then |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qualifying questions | 5 working days | once more after 7 more, then close |
| Interest and times | 3 working days | once more, then close |
| A confirmed call time | no follow-up — confirm the day before only if asked | — |
| After a call they said they'd come back | the date they named, or 5 working days | twice, then close |

Two follow-ups, then close the thread as `closed` with the reason ("no answer
after two follow-ups"). A recruiter who resurfaces reopens it by writing.

## Pace of the actor itself

- One sweep reads at most 25 threads. More than that is a backlog to work
  through over several sessions, not in one burst.
- One run sends at most 10 messages, one thread at a time, with a pause of
  several seconds between sends. A burst of identical-looking activity is what
  platforms flag.
- No sweeps on a timer faster than a few times a day. New recruiter messages
  do not need a minute-level response, and every read is a session on the
  user's account.

## Closing

Close a thread when:

- it was sales or a scam pattern (at once);
- the user declined (as `declined`);
- two follow-ups went unanswered;
- the role was filled or withdrawn;
- it moved to the job-hunter pipeline and nothing more will be said here — keep
  it `scheduled` instead if the conversation continues in the same thread.
